Request a demo

In a short demo we'll show you how to register a customer, her measurements and her receipt, how to follow payments and orders, and the owner dashboard. And we'll answer your questions about your shop.

Request a demo on WhatsApp Request a demo by email

To prepare the demo for you, send us:

  • Your shop's name and city or country.
  • Roughly how many branches you have.
  • How many staff will use the app.
  • The device you use (iPhone or Android).
  • A convenient time for the demo.

Please don't send us your customers' data in your message. A description of your shop is enough.

Can anyone sign up for the app?

There's no self sign-up. We set up your shop's account with you, then the shop owner creates accounts for their staff.

How much does it cost?

Get in touch and, after the demo, we'll agree on what suits the size of your shop.
