We know a shop's day from the inside

Many abaya shops still run on a notebook, a pen and a good memory. It works… until something goes missing.

1

Paper receipts get lost

The order book gets wet or disappears, your customer asks about her order, and nobody knows its number.

2

Measurements on scattered slips

Your customer comes back a year later and her measurements are on a slip nobody can find. So you measure her all over again.

3

Balances nobody remembers

A deposit here, a payment there, and by the end of the month you don't know how much your customers still owe you.

4

Orders run late without you noticing

The delivery date passes, and a finished order waits on the shelf while its owner has no idea it's ready.

Darjina brings all of this together in one place, organised and clear for the whole shop.

All features in detail

How does Darjina work?

Three steps at the counter, with your customer right in front of you.

  1. 1

    Type the mobile number

    A registered customer appears instantly. A new one is added with her name and number.

  2. 2

    Record the measurements and the receipt

    Measurements on the abaya picture, then the price, deposit, number of pieces and delivery date.

  3. 3

    Send the receipt and track the order

    The receipt reaches your customer on WhatsApp, and the order stays on the list until delivery day.

For the shop owner and the team, each with what they need

For the shop owner

  • Adds branches, creates staff accounts and sets their passwords.
  • Changes the price when needed, with a reason, and every change is kept.
  • Records refunds, and corrects an order's status or restores it after cancellation.
  • Follows the dashboard: balances due, late orders, money collected and team performance.
  • Sets the shop's currency, country code, default completion time and language.

For counter staff

  • Registers the customer, her measurements and the receipt quickly, with no paper.
  • Adds payments and updates the order status through to delivery.
  • Sees the "My day" panel: her branch's orders and the money she collected today.
  • Follows her own figures, with an anonymous comparison against the best and lowest performers in the shop.

Owner dashboard

Your whole shop at a glance

Instead of adding up figures from notebooks, the dashboard gives you the full picture. Tap any figure to open the orders or payments behind it.

  • Balances your customers still owe, in each receipt's currency.
  • Late orders, orders due today, and ready orders not yet collected.
  • Money collected by branch, payment method and staff member.
  • Team performance, plus cancellations, refunds and price changes.
  • New and returning customers, and trends across weeks and months.
  • Orders assigned to tailors and each tailor's workload.
  • A daily summary you send on WhatsApp, plus PDF and CSV export.

Built for your shop, and respectful of your customers' privacy

Each shop's data is its own

Every shop is completely separate from the others. No one sees another shop's data, and this protection is enforced in the database itself, not just on screen.

Receipts that keep private details private

The PDF receipt shows only your customer's name and mobile number. Her ID number and date of birth never appear on it.

Arabic first

The app was designed in Arabic and right-to-left from the start, with English available for those who need it.

Nothing gets erased

Payments are never edited or deleted; corrections are made with a clear refund or reversal entry. And every price change has a reason and a date.

Frequently asked questions

Is Darjina suitable for a small shop?

Yes. It works for a shop with one branch and one staff member, and it grows with you as you open new branches.

Which devices does it run on?

It's available now on iPhone through TestFlight, and the Android version is ready. It needs an internet connection while you work.

Does it support my country's currency?

Yes. Each shop has its own currency: Bahraini dinar by default, as well as Saudi riyal, UAE dirham and others, and every receipt keeps the currency it was created in.

Can staff see everything?

No. Staff record and follow up orders and payments, while price changes, refunds and the full dashboard are for the shop owner.

Who can see my customers' data?

Only your shop's team. Every shop is separate from the others, and we don't sell or share data.
