## section#steps

- steps.items:

  - steps.step1.id: arrive
    steps.step1.number: 1
    steps.step1.title: Your customer arrives… type her number
    steps.step1.text: On the home screen, type your customer's mobile number. If she's registered with your shop, she appears as you type and you select her. If she's new, you add her name and number with the country code, and she's created in the same step.
    steps.step1.tip: The search covers your shop's customers only.
    steps.step1.image: [Screenshot: the mobile number field with results appearing while typing]
    steps.step1.image_alt: Searching for a customer by mobile number in Darjina

  - steps.step2.id: measure
    steps.step2.number: 2
    steps.step2.title: Measurements on the abaya picture
    steps.step2.text: If your customer has earlier measurements, they appear ready for you to review or update. If she's new, you enter each measurement in its place on the picture, in centimetres: length, shoulder, bust, waist, hips, sleeve and armhole.
    steps.step2.tip: Old measurements are never erased; they stay in her history.
    steps.step2.image: [Screenshot: the abaya picture with measurement fields]
    steps.step2.image_alt: Entering measurements on the abaya picture in Darjina

  - steps.step3.id: receipt
    steps.step3.number: 3
    steps.step3.title: Receipt details
    steps.step3.text: The price, number of pieces, order description and expected delivery date, which the app suggests based on your shop's completion time. The receipt number is assigned automatically.
    steps.step3.image: [Screenshot: the receipt form]
    steps.step3.image_alt: Receipt details form in Darjina

  - steps.step4.id: deposit
    steps.step4.number: 4
    steps.step4.title: The deposit
    steps.step4.text: Record what your customer paid today. The app calculates the balance instantly and saves the payment with its date, branch and who received it.
    steps.step4.image: [Screenshot: adding a payment]
    steps.step4.image_alt: Recording a deposit in Darjina

  - steps.step5.id: share
    steps.step5.number: 5
    steps.step5.title: The receipt on WhatsApp
    steps.step5.text: One tap creates a PDF receipt with your shop's logo, name and number, sent to your customer on WhatsApp or any app you choose. The receipt shows only her name and mobile number.
    steps.step5.image: [Image: a PDF receipt in a WhatsApp chat]
    steps.step5.image_alt: Sharing a Darjina PDF receipt on WhatsApp

  - steps.step6.id: follow
    steps.step6.number: 6
    steps.step6.title: Tracking while it's being sewn
    steps.step6.text: The order appears in the orders list by delivery date. The shop owner can assign it to a tailor. If your customer comes back with another payment, it's recorded in the receipt's history. When the work is finished, the order is marked "Completed" with the actual completion date.
    steps.step6.tip: Late orders and orders due today show up on the owner dashboard.
    steps.step6.image: [Screenshot: the orders list]
    steps.step6.image_alt: Orders list in Darjina sorted by delivery date

  - steps.step7.id: deliver
    steps.step7.number: 7
    steps.step7.title: Delivery
    steps.step7.text: Your customer collects her abaya; you record the final payment and the "Paid in full" badge appears. Then you change the status to "Delivered". If a balance remains, the order can still be delivered after a confirmation message, and the balance stays visible.
    steps.step7.image: [Screenshot: receipt details with the "Delivered" status and the "Paid in full" badge]
    steps.step7.image_alt: A receipt with the Delivered status and the paid in full badge in Darjina

## section#exceptions

- exceptions.title: And if something changes?
- exceptions.items:
  - exceptions.cancel.title: Cancelling an order
    exceptions.cancel.text: An order is cancelled with a reason. Staff can cancel a pending order; the shop owner can also cancel a completed one and can restore a cancelled order. A delivered order cannot be cancelled.
  - exceptions.price.title: Changing the price
    exceptions.price.text: The shop owner changes the price with a reason, and the change is kept in the history. The price can never be lower than the amount already paid.
  - exceptions.refund.title: Refunding money
    exceptions.refund.text: The shop owner records a refund, or reverses a payment entered by mistake, as a new entry, so the history stays complete and clear.
  - exceptions.mistake.title: A status set by mistake
    exceptions.mistake.text: Status never moves backwards on its own; the shop owner corrects it when needed.
