## section#roles

- roles.title: For the shop owner and the team, each with what they need
- roles.owner.title: For the shop owner
- roles.owner.items:
  - Adds branches, creates staff accounts and sets their passwords.
  - Changes the price when needed, with a reason, and every change is kept.
  - Records refunds, and corrects an order's status or restores it after cancellation.
  - Follows the dashboard: balances due, late orders, money collected and team performance.
  - Sets the shop's currency, country code, default completion time and language.
- roles.worker.title: For counter staff
- roles.worker.items:
  - Registers the customer, her measurements and the receipt quickly, with no paper.
  - Adds payments and updates the order status through to delivery.
  - Sees the "My day" panel: her branch's orders and the money she collected today.
  - Follows her own figures, with an anonymous comparison against the best and lowest performers in the shop.

## section#faq

- faq.title: Frequently asked questions
- faq.items:
  - faq.q1: Is Darjina suitable for a small shop?
    faq.a1: Yes. It works for a shop with one branch and one staff member, and it grows with you as you open new branches.
  - faq.q2: Which devices does it run on?
    faq.a2: It's available now on iPhone through TestFlight, and the Android version is ready. It needs an internet connection while you work.
  - faq.q3: Does it support my country's currency?
    faq.a3: Yes. Each shop has its own currency: Bahraini dinar by default, as well as Saudi riyal, UAE dirham and others, and every receipt keeps the currency it was created in.
  - faq.q4: Can staff see everything?
    faq.a4: No. Staff record and follow up orders and payments, while price changes, refunds and the full dashboard are for the shop owner.
  - faq.q5: Who can see my customers' data?
    faq.a5: Only your shop's team. Every shop is separate from the others, and we don't sell or share data.
  - faq.q6: How much is the subscription?
    faq.a6: Chat with us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a demo for you, then agree on what suits your shop.
